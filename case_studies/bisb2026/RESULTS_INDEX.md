moving them or changing script output paths.

## B1 Double-Peak Binning And Manual-Window Diagnostics

- `paper_results/590_gui_history_area_260506_b1_double_peak_binning_260508/`
- `paper_results/n0_PL2_10w_gui_history_area_260506_b1_double_peak_binning_260508/`
- `paper_results/no_PL2_20w_2film_gui_history_area_260506_highq_refined_b1_double_peak_binning_260508/`
  - Generated by `run_b1_double_peak_binning_analysis("all", runFits=false)`.
  - Main manual-window diagnostics:
    `b1_double_peak_stacked_spectra_positive_q.png`,
    `b1_double_peak_stacked_spectra_negative_q.png`,
    `b1_double_peak_stacked_spectra_absq_combined.png`,
    `b1_double_peak_combined_q_stacked_spectra.png`, and
    `b1_double_peak_manual_window_seed.csv`.
  - Waterfall-style visual stacks matching the manual peak-picking reference:
    `b1_double_peak_waterfall_signed_q.png`,
    `b1_double_peak_waterfall_absq_combined.png`, and
    `b1_double_peak_waterfall_combined_q.png`.
  - Plotting provenance is recorded in
    `b1_double_peak_plot_q_binning_map.csv`; display traces follow the same
    per-dataset noise decision as extraction, so high-SNR points stay
    single-q while low-SNR points use the recorded combined-q bins.
    The smallest-|q| protection region stays single-q even when its local
    noise exceeds the auto threshold, to avoid losing low-q structure.
  - `b1_double_peak_noise_profile.csv` records the per-dataset automatic
    noise threshold, threshold source, and whether each q point was protected
    from binning.
  - These outputs are for B1 lower/upper window selection before the next
    constrained double-peak re-extraction pass.
- `paper_results/b1_double_peak_lower_physical_fit_binning_260508/`
- `paper_results/b1_double_peak_upper_physical_fit_binning_260508/`
- `paper_results/b1_double_peak_lower_physical_fit_enhancements_binning_260508/`
